# Active Recall

**Category:** [[Meta-Learning]] · [[Study Tools]]
**Related:** [[Meta-Learning/Feynman Technique]]

## Practice

Instead of rereading passively, close the source and retrieve the idea from memory. Use questions, flashcards, short summaries, or a blank-page explanation.

## JARVIS application

JARVIS can turn a note into a small quiz, ask one question at a time, track weak areas, and link missed concepts back to their source notes.
