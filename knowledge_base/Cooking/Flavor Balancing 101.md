# Flavor Balancing 101

**Category:** [[Cooking]] · [[Kitchen Basics]]
**Related:** [[Cooking/Maillard Reaction]] · [[Recipes]]

## Core elements

- **Salt** enhances existing flavors and suppresses bitterness.
- **Acid** such as lemon or vinegar cuts richness and brightens dull dishes.
- **Fat** carries flavor and adds mouthfeel.
- **Heat** from spices or peppers adds contrast and excitement.

## Quick fixes

- **Too salty:** add acid, a touch of sweetness, or dilute with unsalted ingredients.
- **Too bland:** try acid or aromatics before adding more salt.

## JARVIS application

For cooking advice, JARVIS should diagnose the imbalance first, recommend a small adjustment, and explain why. It should mention uncertainty when the dish, quantity, or current seasoning is unknown.
